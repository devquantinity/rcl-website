Labor & Employment

With the constant transformation of labor relations and the evolution of labor laws, companies must be prepared to adapt to the new realities of the job market when dealing with issues such as contract modalities, remuneration, working hours, compliance, collective negotiations, internal policies, and training. Additionally, in a context of continuous changes in case law, they must stay updated in their judicial and administrative litigation practices. Structured in a comprehensive, technical, and creative approach, we integrate specialists from various areas of law to strengthen preventive action in matters related to labor relations and the efficient management of labor liabilities.

With a unique combination of legal expertise, multidisciplinary knowledge, and a global vision, we provide solid, innovative, and customized strategies for successful workforce management.

Among the topics we address are:

Litigation

  • Individual and Company litigation: strategic labor lawsuits;
  • Management of strategic case portfolios, combining litigation and advisory to reduce liability;

Advisory

  • Structuring, implementing, and managing compensation and benefits policies, including pay equity;
  • Analysis and structuring of stock option plans;
  • Consulting and training on compliance with health and safety regulations and standards;
  • Development of workplace policies;
  • Training on workplace conduct and prevention of conflicts between employers and employees or among employees of different hierarchies – anti-harassment training, labor inspection compliance training;
  • Consulting on intellectual property protection;
