## 8. Approvals only RLC can give

The Legal Profession (Publicity) Rules 2025 came into force on 1 January 2026 and replaced the 2001 Rules. Publicity must not be misleading, must not contain unverifiable information, and must not create unjustified expectations; a firm may describe itself as specialising in an area where that is justified, but not as an "expert".

We are not in a position to rule on any of this — you are. The template we are building on ships with placeholder marketing content, and we will not publish any of the following without your written approval.

| Item | Our position | Need from you | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Client testimonials | The template ships with invented quotes attributed to invented people. We have removed the review markup that search engines read. | Real quotations with the client's written consent, or instruction to drop the testimonials page. | ☐ To confirm |
| Client names and logos | Not published. | Written consent from each client, or confirmation we leave them off. | ☐ To confirm |
| Case studies and deal highlights | The template's are fictional. | Anonymised matter summaries you are content to publish, or instruction to drop the page. | ☐ To confirm |
| Wording that implies ranking or quality | Phrases such as "leading" or "best" are not in our copy. | Confirm the tone you want, and flag any wording in our draft you are not comfortable with. | ☐ To confirm |
| "12+ industries served" on the home page | Taken from the twelve industries listed in your own profile. | Confirm it is accurate and that you are happy to state it. | ☐ To confirm |
