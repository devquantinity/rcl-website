Founder & Partner

Ezra Toh

Ezra is the Founder and Partner of Rhu Law Chambers (RLC). He graduated from the University of Liverpool and was subsequently admitted as an Advocate and Solicitor of the High Court of Malaya. Having gained valuable professional experience working with a Japanese law firm, Ezra brings a broader perspective to his legal practice, particularly in understanding the commercial needs of both local and international businesses.

His practice also encompasses real estate transactions, including property acquisitions and disposals, property joint ventures, sale-and-leaseback arrangements and commercial leasing.

With a strong focus on cross-border transactions and foreign investments, Ezra has advised and assisted numerous international clients in establishing and expanding their business presence in Malaysia. He also advises emerging businesses and established corporations on equity investments, fundraising arrangements, business expansion and corporate governance. His clients span various industries, including technology, food and beverage, manufacturing, education, real estate and healthcare.

Beyond his legal practice, Ezra is passionate about helping business owners appreciate the importance of sound legal foundations in building successful and sustainable businesses. He actively participates in seminars and business forums, sharing practical legal knowledge and insights with entrepreneurs and business leaders.

Guided by the belief that a lawyer should be more than a legal adviser, Ezra strives to be a trusted strategic partner to his clients, providing practical, commercially driven and solution-oriented legal advice that protects their interests while supporting their long-term business growth.

Seow Swang

Seow Swang is an experienced business consultant, coach and trainer with over 20 years of professional experience in corporate transformation, change management, organisational development and business performance improvement.

Having held key leadership and consulting roles with PwC Malaysia and the PwC Asia Pacific network, Seow Swang brings extensive experience in advising organisations on strategic transformation, leadership development, operational effectiveness and organisational change.

Her expertise encompasses mergers and acquisitions integration, corporate restructuring and business consolidation, particularly in managing organisational transitions, aligning business operations and strengthening corporate performance. She has worked with both public and private sector organisations, including Intel, Heineken, Manulife, PETRONAS, Sunway Medical Centre and Bursa Malaysia.

As a Consultant of Rhu Law Chambers, Seow Swang contributes valuable commercial and organisational perspectives, complementing the firm’s corporate and commercial legal practice. Her experience strengthens the firm’s understanding of the operational and strategic challenges businesses face during acquisitions, restructuring, expansion and organisational transformation.

June Lu

June Lu is an experienced international consultant with over 22 years of expertise in education planning, cross-border immigration and international advisory services. Originally from Malaysia, she has resided in the United Kingdom for more than 30 years, providing her with a unique understanding of international business environments, regulatory frameworks and cross-cultural market dynamics.

Throughout her career, June has successfully assisted numerous Asian families in relocating and settling in the United Kingdom, offering comprehensive services encompassing immigration planning, property acquisition, children’s education, university admissions and bespoke concierge arrangements. Her expertise enables her to develop integrated and seamless overseas relocation solutions tailored to the needs of her clients.

Beyond immigration and education, June possesses extensive experience in international trade and cross-border business development. She has assisted Chinese enterprises in expanding into European markets and supported European businesses entering China by leveraging her international business network and in-depth market knowledge. Her work has positioned her as a trusted adviser in facilitating commercial opportunities between Eastern and Western markets.

As the founder of Green Rich (M) Sdn. Bhd., June focuses on creating cross-border investment opportunities between Malaysia and the United Kingdom, particularly through the Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) programme. Her advisory services encompass relocation planning, investment strategies, education pathways and lifestyle arrangements, providing clients with comprehensive solutions for international mobility and long-term settlement.

With her extensive industry experience, international network and client-centric approach, June is committed to connecting business opportunities, facilitating cross-border investments and delivering practical, tailored solutions to individuals, families and businesses pursuing international opportunities.

Shew Mee Ling

Shew is the Founder and Director of V Act Management Services Sdn. Bhd., with over a decade of experience in corporate secretarial practice, corporate governance and regulatory compliance. As a Fellow of the Malaysian Institute of Chartered Secretaries and Administrators (MAICSA), she has extensive experience advising business owners, directors and companies on corporate compliance, statutory obligations and corporate governance matters.

Throughout her career, Shew has worked closely with small and medium enterprises (SMEs), entrepreneurs and corporate directors, providing practical guidance on company incorporation, corporate administration, statutory compliance and corporate governance. Her business-oriented approach enables clients to navigate regulatory requirements effectively while establishing sound corporate structures to support sustainable business growth.

Beyond her professional practice, Shew is a former President of BNI Focus Shah Alam, where she demonstrated strong leadership in fostering professional relationships, business collaboration and strategic networking among entrepreneurs and business leaders.

With her combined expertise in corporate compliance, governance and business advisory, Shew is committed to helping businesses maintain strong regulatory foundations, enhance corporate governance practices and pursue long-term commercial growth.

Teh Ping Ying

With over 22 years of experience in the financial services industry, Ping Ying is an experienced wealth and estate planning professional specialising in wealth preservation, estate and legacy planning, business succession, retirement planning and trust solutions.

Prior to entering the financial and estate planning industry, she served as a Buyer and Planner at Western Digital, where she developed valuable expertise in strategic planning, risk management and operational management. Her combined corporate and financial experience enables her to provide practical and comprehensive wealth planning solutions tailored to the needs of business owners, professionals and families.

She holds the Registered Financial Planner (RFP) qualification and is a qualified Life Advisor. Her professional achievements include the Rockwills CEO Award, Million Dollar Round Table (MDRT) qualification and 15 consecutive years of recognition through the Supremacy Awards, reflecting her longstanding commitment to professional excellence and client service.

Throughout her career, she has worked closely with small and medium enterprise (SME) owners, high-net-worth individuals and families, advising on wealth protection, business continuity, succession planning and intergenerational wealth transfer.

Alexandra S. Yi

Alexandra S. Yi is a Taiwan-qualified attorney based in Taipei, holding a Master of Laws (LL.M.) from National Taiwan University. She specialises in civil and commercial litigation, dispute resolution, and cross-border legal matters, advising both individual and corporate clients on complex legal issues involving multiple jurisdictions.

Her practice focuses on cross-border disputes, including jurisdictional assessments, litigation strategy, international legal coordination, and the recognition and enforcement of foreign judgments. She has experience assisting overseas clients in navigating legal proceedings in Taiwan and addressing matters involving parties, assets and evidence across different jurisdictions.

With a strong understanding of the legal complexities arising from international transactions and disputes, Alexandra adopts a strategic and practical approach to legal problem-solving. She is committed to providing effective legal solutions, facilitating seamless coordination across jurisdictions, and safeguarding her clients’ interests in cross-border legal matters.

Partner (Thailand-Based)

Tipsuda “May” Watabunditkul

Tipsuda “May” Watabunditkul is a Thailand-qualified lawyer based in Udon Thani, holding a Master of Laws (LL.M.) and Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) from Ramkhamhaeng University, Thailand. She has been licensed to practise law in Thailand since 2015.

Her practice focuses on corporate and commercial transactions, contract drafting and negotiation, employment law, property and land matters, debt recovery, and dispute resolution. She also advises on family law and inheritance matters, and has experience assisting foreign investors, entrepreneurs, and international clients in navigating Thailand’s legal and regulatory framework, including matters involving cross-border business activities and commercial disputes.

With a strong understanding of the Thai legal landscape and the practical challenges faced by businesses and international clients, May adopts a strategic, practical, and commercially oriented approach to legal advisory. She is committed to providing effective legal solutions, facilitating cross-border business activities, resolving legal disputes, and safeguarding her clients’ legal and commercial interests in Thailand.
